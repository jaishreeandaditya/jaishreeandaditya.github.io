Chapter three

Nearby Destinations

If you have some extra time, you can also explore two of India's most iconic destinations:

≈ 3–4 hrs by road

Ayodhya

Visit the Ram Mandir and explore the sacred city.

≈ 5–6 hrs by road

Varanasi

Experience the Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat, visit the Kashi Vishwanath Temple and explore the city's famous ghats.

A little tip

Make sure to save some room for Lucknow's famous kebabs, chaats, biryani and desserts. The city's food is an experience in itself!
